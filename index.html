How the scores are calculated

Sector score = Quality × 40% + Growth × 35% + Timing × 25%. Each is a 0–100 input you supply per sector.

Company score combines four sub-scores:

  • Profitability (35%) — average of (ROE ÷ 30 × 100) and (ROCE ÷ 30 × 100), each capped at 100.
  • Growth (30%) — (3-year revenue growth ÷ 25 × 100), capped at 100.
  • Balance sheet (20%) — 100 − (debt/equity ÷ 1.5 × 100), capped at 0–100.
  • Valuation (15%) — 50 − (PE premium or discount vs. sector, in percentage points).

Final score = Profitability × 0.35 + Growth × 0.30 + Balance sheet × 0.20 + Valuation × 0.15, rounded to a whole number.

Bands: 70+ strong candidate · 50–69 watchlist · below 50 reject.

Banks don't fit that formula — ROCE and debt/equity aren't meaningful when deposits are 30-40x equity by design. Banks use a variant instead: Profitability from ROE alone (35%), Growth from revenue growth (30%), Asset quality from Net NPA % instead of debt/equity (20%), Valuation unchanged (15%).
